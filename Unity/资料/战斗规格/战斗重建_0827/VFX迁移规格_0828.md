## 1. 事件文本→VFX 映射总表（要点，明细见子代理报告全文）

**A. 死亡爆散**：`"%s destroyed"`(L4567)→Actual_Explosion(3D)/Card 3D Death Explosion(2D)；落点=rule_core L4566 埋的 `ctx["_last_death"]` {p,slot,name} 槽中心（勿用固定 (960,600)）；死亡计数 2/4→`SFX.set_battle_intensity(1/2,arena)`；`"warlord destroyed!"` 不播爆炸。

**B. 战术打出**：`"Player %d played tactic %s"`(L2162)→`CARD_VFX[卡名]`（85 条整体照抄）；旧教训：战术专属 VFX 必须打在 "played tactic" 分支（只随部署播=伤害战术永不触发）。

**C. 部署**：`"Player %d deployed %s to slot %d"`(L2260)/`"Deploy: %s deployed to slot %d"`(L3877)/`"...companion deployed..."`(L2222)→BlueSummonCircle+CARD_VFX[卡名]+`_play_deploy_flight`；**旧槽位解析 `substr(idx+9)`=off-by-one bug（恒 0 号槽），勿抄→`substr(idx+8)` 或正则 `to slot (\d+)`**。

**D. 治疗/再生**：healed/regenerated→Healing_Projectile+阵营变体(_Frost/_Ork/_Green)+BattleHeal；目标=单位名查槽中心。

**E. 眩晕**：stunned→StunEffect_proc+BattleStun。

**G. 关键词获得**：`"%s gained %s"`/`"... gained %s instead"`/`"... gained %s (Synapse spread)"`→GAIN_VFX[rest]（blind/flank/flying/fast/stealth/vulnerable/camouflage/cantattack/pindown/blood thirst/hunt mark/markerlight/long range/sentry/artifice/ecstasy）+GAIN_FAC（invulnerable/stealth 阵营变体）；特判：Armour→ArmourEffect_Bladeguard_Shield+BattleGainArmor / Shield→Shield Enter+BattleShield / Shuriken→GainShuriken+BattleGainShuriken / Long Range→LongRange_GainTrait+BattleLongRange；未命中→Buff_Blue+BattleBuff；`"Player %d gained..."` 前缀过滤防误播。

**H. 伤害/攻击**：`"%s attacked %s: %d damage (after armor)"`(L4295)→ATTACK_VFX[攻击者名]→RANGED_VFX[阵营]（需 `_atk_type_by_name` 回迁）→SLASH_VFX[阵营]+`_show_damage_number`（弹道 `_fx2d(vfx,tgt,from)`+animinfo）；counterattacked→BattleBacklash；Sentry 伤害→Sentry_Effect@攻击者位；markerlight 消耗→MarkerlightSource@目标位；`"hit, lost Long Range"`→LongRange_LoseTrait(2D-only)；`"shield blocked"`/`"invulnerable: immune"`/Stomp overflow/Unstable（旧死代码，目标取末词）→新加分项。

**I. 触发（TRIG）**：`_trigger_fx` L3404 `"%s triggered: %s"` key.capitalize() 21 种；**20 条两库均无→SFX 先行+待转换**；Pray/Ecstasy 不在旧 TRIG_VFX→补 2 条（EcstasyEffect 2D✅；Pray 无→待转换）；`"Ambush revealed: %s"`(L1965)→BattleAmbush。

**J. 其他**：returned to hand→Recall_{Eldar_2_quick_ground,Generic}+CardMove@(960,HAND_Y-50)；`"created %s in player %d hand"`→CREATE_CARD_VFX[来源阵营]；Ferocity 回牌库→Ferocity_RecallToDeck；fatigue→AttackHitSmall@玩家督军位；Skull!→BattleSkull（**SFX_FILES 无此键=旧静默**）；辅助日志（Turn started/chooses/OVERTIME/ephemeral removed 等）不接。
