## 二、统计方法（可复跑）

### 2·1 用量统计

```python
# python -I -X utf8
import json,io,collections
d=json.load(io.open('Unity/资料/普查产出_1018/allcards_1350/cards_raw.json',encoding='utf-8'))
trig=collections.Counter(); trig_cards=collections.defaultdict(set)
eff=collections.Counter();  eff_cards=collections.defaultdict(set)
for c in d['cards']:
    for ca in c.get('cardAbilities') or []:
        trig[ca['trigger']]+=1; trig_cards[ca['trigger']].add(c['uniqueId'])
    ls=[al for ca in (c.get('cardAbilities') or []) for al in (ca.get('abilityLogic') or [])]
    for k in ('activeAbility','alternativeAbility'):
        ls += ((c.get(k) or {}).get('activeAbilityLogic') or [])
    for al in ls:
        eff[al['chosenAbility']]+=1; eff_cards[al['chosenAbility']].add(c['uniqueId'])
print('triggers used',len(trig),'effects used',len(eff),
      'trig total',sum(trig.values()),'logic total',sum(eff.values()))
```

输出：`triggers used 60 effects used 57 trig total 1496 logic total 2078`。
