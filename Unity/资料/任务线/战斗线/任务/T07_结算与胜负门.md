|---|---|---|
| **谁是胜负门** | `BattleManager.DeadHero(playerIsLoser, BattleResult)` —— **唯一入口**，70+ 个 `Resolve*` 收尾都走到它 | ✅ `RuleCore.CheckWinner` |
