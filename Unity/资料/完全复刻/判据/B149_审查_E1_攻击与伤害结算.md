`Check(DeathTypeOfSource(TacticSourceCtx()) == 30)` ∧ `Check(DeathTypeOfSource(UnitSourceCtx()) == 20)` ∧ `Check(DeathTypeOfSource(NoSourceCtx()) == 0)` ∧ `Check(档1 != 档2)`。
